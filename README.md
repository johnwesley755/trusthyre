## 📖 About TrustHyre

**TrustHyre** is an on-demand workforce marketplace created to solve the three biggest challenges in India's skilled trade, frontline, and blue-collar employment sectors:
1. **Worker No-Shows & Attendance Disputes**: Employers often face unreliable staffing and unverified punch-ins.
2. **Credential & Identity Fraud**: Lack of verification leads to unvetted personnel on job sites.
3. **Delayed Wage Payments**: Frontline workers frequently experience delayed wages, arbitrary deductions, and lack of pay transparency.

TrustHyre solves these issues through **verified candidate profiles**, **geofenced shift clock-in/out**, **escrow-backed wage protection**, and **curated job discovery**.

## 🌟 Key Application Features

### 👷 1. For Candidates & Skilled Workers

* **Quick Mobile Onboarding**: Sign up in seconds with Indian mobile number verification (`+91`), choose your trade category, preferred city, and experience level.
* **Trust Badge & Verified Profile**: Activate your Trust Badge to rank higher in recruiter searches and prove your credentials to hiring companies.
* **Curated Job Search & One-Click Apply**: Browse verified local jobs across top trade categories. Apply immediately with your complete verified profile—no manual paperwork needed.
* **Transparent Wages & Shifts**: Every job displays clear pay rates (hourly, daily, or monthly), shift hours, benefits, and exact worksite locations upfront.
* **Geofenced Shift Clock-In**: Start and end your shifts directly on your mobile device. Built-in GPS verification confirms your attendance at the worksite coordinates, eliminating paper registers and attendance disputes.
* **Guaranteed Escrow Payouts**: Because employers pre-fund shifts into a secure escrow vault, your earnings are protected and released directly to your account upon shift sign-off.
* **Professional PDF Resume Generator**: Download a standardized, professional resume complete with your verified trade badges, work history, ratings, and a scannable verification QR code.

---

### 🏢 2. For Employers & Recruiters

* **Instant Job Posting**: Create detailed job listings and emergency shift requests within minutes. Specify required skills, headcount, hourly/monthly pay, and worksite locations.
* **Verified Candidate Discovery**: Search through pre-screened talent across India. Filter by city, trade category, verified skill level (Levels 1 to 5), and past reliability ratings.
* **Real-Time Applicant Tracking (ATS)**: Manage incoming applications in one dashboard. Review candidate credentials, skill levels, and schedule interviews or direct shift dispatches.
* **Pre-Funded Escrow Vault**: Deposit shift compensation into a secure platform escrow before workers deploy, providing transparency that attracts the highest-quality candidates.
* **Live Attendance & Geofenced Verification**: Monitor when workers arrive on-site with tamper-proof geofenced punch-in verification. Review completed hours and approve wage disbursements with a single click.
* **Multi-Location Management**: Manage staffing across multiple job sites, warehouses, restaurant branches, construction sites, and corporate facilities from a centralized company account.

---

### 🔍 3. Intelligent Job Discovery & Verification Engine

* **Multi-Portal Aggregation**: Continuously searches verified public recruitment feeds and partner boards to aggregate genuine blue-collar and frontline opportunities across all major Indian industrial hubs.
* **Strict Anti-Spam & Deduplication**: Cleans duplicate job posts across multiple sources and filters out misleading offers or substandard wages.
* **Admin Verification Gate**: All discovered job postings undergo administrative review and moderation before being shown to candidates, ensuring workers only see legitimate, verified employment.

---

### 🛡️ 4. Administration & Compliance Hub

* **Candidate Verification Desk**: Platform administrators review pending credential verifications and manage trust confidence ratings.
* **Job Moderation Console**: Dedicated tools to review, approve, edit, or reject scraped and user-submitted job postings.
* **Employer Lead Management**: Ingests, dedupes, and prioritizes enterprise employer leads to expand hiring options for candidates.
* **Dispute Resolution & Audit Trails**: Tamper-proof logs of shift timers, GPS check-ins, and escrow approvals to resolve any on-site discrepancies equitably.
